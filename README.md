# Opria AI

> Understand what your business needs. Find the right people to build it.

Opria is an AI-powered business growth and professional matching platform designed to help businesses understand their real challenges before connecting them with suitable professionals.

Instead of asking a business owner to already know exactly what they need, Opria starts by understanding the business.

A business may say:

> "I want to grow my business online."

But that does not automatically mean the business simply needs a website.

Depending on the business, it may need e-commerce, payment integration, inventory management, automation, branding, digital marketing, analytics, or a combination of different services.

Opria uses AI to help transform an unclear business need into a structured requirement and then recommends suitable professionals based on skills, experience, portfolio relevance, industry experience, availability, and project compatibility.

---

## The Problem

Many businesses know that they want to grow or solve a problem, but they often do not know:

- What type of solution they actually need
- Which technologies or services are relevant
- What questions they should ask
- How to define their project requirements
- Which professional is genuinely suitable for their needs

At the same time, skilled developers, designers, marketers, and other professionals often spend significant time searching for clients instead of focusing on the work they specialize in.

Traditional freelance platforms usually expect the client to:

1. Know what they need
2. Create an account
3. Write a project description
4. Search through professionals
5. Compare proposals
6. Select someone

Opria approaches the problem differently.
